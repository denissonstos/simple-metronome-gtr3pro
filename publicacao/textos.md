# Textos para a loja da Zepp

Limites do console: nome até 30, introdução até 40, detalhes até 600 caracteres.

## English (Default)

**App Name:** Simple Metronome

**App Introduction:**
Feel the beat on your wrist.

**App Details:**
A simple metronome for practicing without looking at your watch. On every beat the watch vibrates and an orange ring flashes.

- 30 to 250 BPM, set with the crown or the - and + buttons (hold to scroll)
- Tempo marking on screen, from Grave to Prestissimo
- Steady beat: delays never add up
- Opens at the last BPM used
- Screen stays on while playing

Note: raising your arm switches the screen off and pauses the beat; the watch does not allow apps to prevent this. Keep "Raise wrist to wake" on: when you look at the watch again, the metronome resumes by itself.

## Privacy Statement

Simple Metronome does not collect, transmit or share any data. The app does not use the internet, does not access health sensors or location, and requests no permissions. It stores only two values, kept in the watch's memory: the last BPM you chose, so the app opens at the same tempo next time, and the time of the last beat, so it can resume playing after the screen switches off. These values never leave the watch.

## Features Descriptions

Vibration metronome. Each beat triggers a short vibration and flashes an orange ring. BPM 30-250, adjusted with the crown or on-screen - and + buttons; the tempo marking (Grave to Prestissimo) is shown. Keeps the screen on while playing and resumes by itself if the watch switches the screen off. The last BPM and the time of the last beat are stored locally on the watch. No network, no sensors, no permissions.
